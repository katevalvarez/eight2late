the concept

eight2late is a collection of all the events hosted in bethany, brianna, kate, & raylan’s spelman 82 dorm.

from pop-up cafes, birthday parties, and kamayans, we hope spelman 82 becomes a space for our community at princeton to exist and enjoy each other’s company.
